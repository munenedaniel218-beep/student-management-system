Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset option on the login page or contact technical support.

Where can I view my fee balance?

Open the Finance section from your student dashboard to view your fee statement and balance.

Where can I find my exam timetable?

Your latest examination timetable is available under the Exams section.

Why can't I see one of my courses?

Your course may not have been registered yet. Contact academic support if the problem continues.
